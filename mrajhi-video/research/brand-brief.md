## What they offer (value proposition per line)
**Real-estate services** (/services)
1. التطوير العقاري — high-quality residential units built to precise engineering specs, hands-on supervision, periodic progress reports; "balance between cost and quality".
2. إدارة الممتلكات — own + third-party properties: marketing, maintenance, renovation, rent collection, periodic financial and market reports to support owners' investment decisions.
3. تأجير العقارات — innovative residential designs in key Riyadh districts and across the Kingdom; also Cairo, Dubai, Bosnia & Herzegovina.

**Contracting & construction** (/contracting) — slogan **«الراجحي، شعارنا الجودة أولاً»**
1. المقاولات العامة — engineering/management/skilled labour teams; dozens of projects in and outside KSA; direct supervision + periodic reports; supplier/agent network keeps quality high and cost reasonable.
2. الاستشارات الهندسية — architectural planning, interior design, facades; optimal use of space; "turning ideas into reality".
3. الصيانة والتشغيل — long-term maintenance & operations contracts for residential/commercial complexes, hotels.

**Rental listings** (/units) — apartments, villas, floors, offices, shops for annual rent (prices shown in SAR/yr; volatile → not used in the video).

## Inferred target audience (conclusion)
**Primary:** Riyadh residents — Saudi and expat families and working professionals (mid-to-upper income: unit rents SAR 45k–125k/yr, 1–4 bedroom apartments and villas in Al-Olaya, Al-Sulaymaniyah, Al-Malaz, Al-Murabba) looking for a *trustworthy, quality* annual rental and a hassle-free process. **Secondary:** property owners/investors (property management, "priority to our investors") and project owners needing contracting/consulting. The site's emphasis on verified listings, transparent pricing and support (vs. luxury/status) tells us the emotional lever is **confidence and comfort** ("home"), not exclusivity.
