# Creative direction — «الراجحي للتنمية والاستثمارات»

Format: 16:9 1920×1080 (55.5 s) + 9:16 1080×1920 (29 s), 30 fps, 120 BPM music grid (1 beat = 15 f, 1 bar = 60 f).

## 1. Three concepts

**A — «خط الأفق الذهبي» (The Golden Skyline)** *(chosen)*
One gold line — lifted straight from the logo's gold tower — is the film's hero. It draws upward in the hook, becomes the wipe between every scene, underlines every headline, traces the growth curve 2000→2026, and finally *assembles the real logo*. Photography stays warm (Riyadh golden hour, sandstone facades) against deep navy. Emotional arc: *doubt → warmth → confidence → belonging.* Style: editorial, restrained, precise; big Arabic type, masked photo reveals, parallax, grain.

**B — «مفتاح البيت» (The Key)** — a literal key-turn / door-opening metaphor per scene. Strong story, but props are not in the brand assets (would need illustration/stock) → violates "built from real assets".

**C — «٢٦ سنة، مبنى بعد مبنى» (Building by Building)** — a timeline where a building appears for every year. Nice with the real 1→41 project data, but photo-to-year mapping isn't on the site (we'd be inventing), and pacing is a repeated device.

**Why A:** it uses only what is real — the logo geometry, real facades, real stats — and the one device (gold line) gives every transition a motivated reason and makes the logo reveal feel *earned* instead of pasted on. It also matches the brand promise «الثقة»: precise, straight, dependable lines.

## 4. Motion system (defined once in `src/theme.ts`)

- **Easings:** `expoOut = bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` (all reveals) · `inOutQuart = bezier(.76,0,.24,1)` (wipes/transitions) · `softOut = bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` (parallax drift) · `linear` only for Ken Burns.
- **Springs:** `smooth {damping:200}` (default UI) · `pop {damping:16, stiffness:140, mass:.7}` (stat numerals, single small overshoot) · never bouncy on type.
- **Durations:** micro 9 f · reveal 18–24 f · scene wipes 15 f · counters 45 f.
- **Stagger:** words 4 f · list items 6 f · cards 8 f. RTL: index 0 = rightmost.
- **Grid / margins (1920×1080):** 12 columns, 120 px side margins, 96 px top/bottom, 24 px gutters. **1080×1920:** 6 columns, 72 px sides, 140 px top/bottom (below/above platform UI zones).
- **Type (final):** display = **Noto Kufi Arabic** 800 (headlines, numerals, card titles) + text = **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** 400–700 (support lines, labels, CTA pills). (Revision: the first cut used the site's own Tajawal; changed on client request for a more premium pairing.) Headline 120 · subhead 64 · body 44 · caption 30 (landscape); portrait sizes in theme.ts. Never letter-spacing on Arabic. Words animate as whole units. Stats use Latin digits exactly like the site's About page.
- **Colour:** navy #0D1B3E base, deeper #070F26 vignettes, gold #DCA50D (logo) for lines/numerals, gold-soft #C9A84C for UI details, warm white #F7F3E8 text, charcoal #38393D only inside logo/plates.
- **Depth & finish:** navy→black radial gradient plate, film grain (SVG feTurbulence seeded per frame, 6 % opacity, mix-blend overlay), soft drop shadows on cards, light sweep (linear gradient mask) on logo & key cards, vignette on photos, ≥ 0.6 scrim under any text over photos for contrast.
- **Photos:** always moving (scale 1.0→1.10 ± 30 px drift, direction alternates), never scaled beyond 1.25× native; low-res (< 1400 px) only inside frames/cards.
- **Audio grid:** 120 BPM; scene cuts on beat pairs (every 30 f); text hits on beats (15 f).
